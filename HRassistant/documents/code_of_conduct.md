# TechNova Solutions - Employee Code of Conduct
Fictional sample document for RAG testing.

- Employees must behave respectfully, honestly, and professionally.
- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, and discrimination are prohibited.
- Workplace concerns should be reported through the official HR grievance channel.
- Retaliation against good-faith reporting is prohibited.
- Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest.
- Confidential information may only be accessed for authorized business purposes.
- Policy violations may be investigated fairly, and corrective action depends on the facts.

FAQ:
Q: Where can I report workplace harassment?
A: Contact HR through the official grievance channel or designated reporting officer.

# TechNova Solutions - Leave and Attendance Policy
Fictional sample document for RAG testing.

- Working hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Casual Leave: 6 days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: 6 days per calendar year.
- Earned Leave: 12 days per calendar year.
- Planned leave should be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
- Emergency or sick leave must be reported to the manager as soon as possible.
- Leave is not considered approved until the manager confirms it.
- Employees must record attendance through the official system.

FAQ:
Q: How many casual leaves are available?
A: Six days per calendar year under this sample policy.

Q: Can I take leave without approval?
A: Employees should obtain approval, except in genuine emergencies that must be reported promptly.

# TechNova Solutions - Salary and Payroll Policy
Fictional sample document for RAG testing.

- Salary is normally processed once per month.
- The target payment date is the last working day of the month, subject to processing conditions.
- Payslips are available through the official employee portal.
- Employees should report payroll errors through the official HR helpdesk.
- Payroll data is confidential.
- The assistant must not disclose individual salary details without authorization.
- Payroll will review reported discrepancies and communicate the outcome.

FAQ:
Q: When is salary normally credited?
A: The target date is the last working day of the month, subject to processing conditions.

Q: How do I report a salary discrepancy?
A: Raise a ticket through the official HR helpdesk.

# TechNova Solutions - Work From Home Policy
Fictional sample document for RAG testing.

- Work from home depends on role, performance, project requirements, and manager approval.
- Employees must submit a request through the official HR system.
- Employees must remain available during agreed working hours.
- Employees must attend scheduled meetings.
- Company devices and approved communication tools must be used.
- Confidential documents must not be shared through personal email or unauthorized storage.
- Lost devices must be reported to IT Security immediately.

FAQ:
Q: Is work from home automatic?
A: No. It requires approval from the reporting manager.

Q: Can I send company documents using personal email?
A: No. Use approved company systems only.
